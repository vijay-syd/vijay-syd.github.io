Who We Are

A Manufacturer You Can Trust

Founded in 2026, Vijay Garments started as a small workshop with big ambitions. Today, we are a full-scale garment manufacturer producing thousands of pieces across men's, women's, and kids' clothing every month.

We work with local and international brands, retailers, and wholesalers who trust us to deliver consistently — on time, every time. Our team is passionate about every stitch, every seam, and every garment that leaves our facility.

From design consultation to final delivery, we manage the entire manufacturing process in-house, giving our clients full confidence in quality and timelines.
